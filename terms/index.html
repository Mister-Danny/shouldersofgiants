Who can use it

  • Students use Shoulders of Giants through a teacher's class, or as a guest without an account. Students under 13 use it with their school's or a parent's permission, which teachers confirm when they sign up.
  • Teachers and other adults who create an account must be 18 or older. By signing up, you confirm that you have your school's permission to use the game with students, or that you're the student's parent or guardian.

What teachers agree to

  • Use the game with students in line with your school's rules.
  • Keep your sign-in, your class codes and your students' login cards private. Give each student only their own login.
  • Remind students not to type their real names or other personal information anywhere in the game, including the Feedback form.
  • Tell us at hello@playshouldersofgiants.com if you think an account has been misused, or if a student or class needs to be deleted.

About the history

Shoulders of Giants teaches history through a game, so some events and people are simplified to make the game playable. We aim to be accurate, but we can get things wrong. If you spot a mistake, please tell us at hello@playshouldersofgiants.com. The game is a teaching aid, not a replacement for a teacher or the curriculum.
