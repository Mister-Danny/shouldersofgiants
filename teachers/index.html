Ancient history your students will actually want to play.

Shoulders of Giants is a free strategy card game where students learn early humans, Mesopotamia and Ancient Egypt by out-playing history's giants. It runs in any browser, with nothing to install and no student emails.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no paid upgrades, and no trial period.

Do students need accounts?

No. Anyone can play as a guest, and guest progress stays in that browser. To save progress across devices and show up on your dashboard, students join your class: they pick a generated historical username and get a password. No email, no real name.

What devices work?

Chromebooks and laptops, in any modern browser. Nothing to install.

How long is a session?

About 15–20 minutes. A single battle takes 5–10 minutes, so it fits at the start or end of a period.

Can I try it before my class does?

Yes. Play it yourself as a guest, no sign-in needed. If you make a teacher account later, what you've played carries over.

What data do you collect?

For students in a class: their generated username and password, game progress, learning-check totals and time played. We never collect student emails or real names, and there are no ads or ad tracking. The details are on the privacy page.
